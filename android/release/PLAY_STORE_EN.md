## Short description

Stretch the colors in your photos into your own pixel stretch artwork.

## Full description

Make your moment last a little longer.

yourcolor turns colors from your photos into pixel stretch artwork. Pick a photo, refine the subject, choose a direction and shape, and save your creation as a JPG.

• Stretch colors up, down, left, or right
• Adjust the stretch length and starting line
• Choose straight, fan, or curved shapes
• Protect or erase subject areas with a brush
• Compare with the original, undo, and redo
• Save finished images to your device as JPGs

Try the editor with the included sample photos. Your selected photos are processed on your device, and the subject separation model is built into the app. No account is needed.

Subject edges may vary by photo. You can refine hair or complex backgrounds with the brush. Saved images have no watermark.
